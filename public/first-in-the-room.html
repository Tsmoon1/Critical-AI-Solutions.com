The Hosts

Two people learning out loud,
so you don't have to learn alone.

Different generations. Different paths. One shared conviction: the best way to learn AI is to pick it up and play.

IL
Ila Lama
Systems Thinker · Storyteller · AI for Impact

Ila is what happens when systems meets storytelling. An IT project management professional with roots in product marketing, brand strategy, creative direction, and community building — she uses all of it. Outside her 9-to-5 she mentors students and professionals, guest lectures, and develops brand and content strategy.

Her specialty: equipping people with the tools, confidence, and voice to own every room they walk into. Her research into AI for Impact explores how these tools shape access, equity, and human connection — because AI is moving fast, and the people it affects most are often the last ones in the room.

At her core, she is a storyteller — through written work, spoken word, and visual art. As a 1.5 gen immigrant and woman of color, storytelling is how she's always made sense of the world.

"How can we build with these tools while still thinking critically, staying emotionally present, and honoring what makes us human?"
TM
Tim Moon
Educator · Rhetorician · AI Consultant · Author

Tim Moon spent decades teaching people how to think — rhetoric, logic, history, argumentation — before AI arrived and handed him the most interesting classroom he's ever walked into. He's the Founder of Critical AI Solutions and an Adjunct Instructor at Wayland Baptist University, where he teaches "Professional Readiness in the Age of AI" to high school seniors, college freshmen, and working adults starting over.

Before AI, Tim served as Headmaster at multiple K-12 institutions across the country, earning accreditations, building programs, and learning that most resistance to change is really just fear with better posture. He holds an M.A. in History from Portland State University and a classical education background that makes him deeply skeptical of hype — and genuinely excited when something actually works.

He's also a published author with books on faith, reason, and the human interior — plus a novel. He writes at timmoon.substack.com. His job on this show: make sure no one gets left at the door.

"The best time to learn this was last year. The second best time is right now — and it's a lot more fun than you think."
